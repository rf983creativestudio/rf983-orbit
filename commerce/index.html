02 / FIRST PRIORITY

Advertising, done responsibly.

Our first target is to prepare RF983 ORBIT for a publisher review. These are planning previews, not live advertisements or Google ad units.

PREPARATION IN PROGRESS

Build trust before serving ads.

We will prioritise useful editorial pages such as Knowledge Universe guides and World Encyclopedia entries. Ads will not be placed beside game controls, download buttons, creative canvases or navigation.

  • Original, substantial and useful content
  • Clear navigation and working pages
  • Accessible privacy and terms information
  • Clearly separated advertising labels
  • No artificial traffic or encouraged clicks
