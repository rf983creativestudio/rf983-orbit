Privacy notice.

This notice explains the limited information practices associated with this website.

1. Information you provide

This website currently does not provide visitor accounts, newsletter registration or contact forms. The word counter, quiz, converter and local clock operate in your browser. The local clock uses your device's time settings; it does not request your location.

2. Website hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical information, such as server logs and network information, to deliver and protect the hosting service. Their own privacy terms apply to that processing.

3. External services

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and may link to external reference websites. When your browser requests third-party resources, those providers may receive technical connection information. External services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Analytics, cookies and local storage

RF983 ORBIT has not intentionally added advertising trackers, analytics tools or visitor-account cookies to this website. Some interactive features save progress, an optional explorer nickname and avatar, collected world stamps, saved Earth Explorer field notes, World Encyclopedia bookmarks and World Database records and Knowledge Constellation trail progress, and private notes in your browser local storage. Science Centre experiment settings and quiz answers are calculated in your browser for the current visit and are not submitted to a server or saved as a profile. This information stays on your device; it is not sent to an RF983 ORBIT account or synced across devices. The website may also use your browser cache and a service worker to store core site files on your device so previously cached pages can load when you are offline. Cached website files are separate from your personal notes and progress. You can clear browser site data to remove local data and cached files. If browser storage is unavailable, some features may not retain progress or work offline.

5. Links to other websites

External websites are provided for reference or convenience. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those services.

6. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated if website features or information practices change. Please check this page for the latest version.

This is a general website privacy notice, not legal advice. It should be reviewed and updated before new interactive features or data collection are introduced.

Creative Studio

The ORBIT Creative Studio creates patterns, pixel art, posters and colour palettes locally in your browser. Artwork is not uploaded to an RF983 ORBIT server by these tools. Exported PNG files are saved through your browser. Temporary drawing undo history exists only during the current page visit.

Sound Lab

ORBIT Sound Lab generates original tones, rhythms and ambient audio locally in your browser using the Web Audio API. It does not stream a music library, record microphone input, upload audio or save listening history. Audio begins only after you interact with a playback control.
