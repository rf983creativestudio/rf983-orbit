A QUICK CREATOR'S GUIDE

Small moves.
Big possibilities.

Animation is simply change over time. Start with one object, set two keyframes and watch the space between them come alive.

01 / COMPOSE

Build a scene

Add circles, squares, stars or text. Choose a preset to begin with a ready-made idea.

02 / ANIMATE

Set keyframes

Choose a moment, adjust your object and set a keyframe. Repeat at another moment.

03 / PREVIEW

Watch it move

Play the timeline to preview smooth transitions between your saved keyframes.

04 / EXPORT

Keep your work

Export a PNG snapshot or record a short WebM animation supported by your browser.
