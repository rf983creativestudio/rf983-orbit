03 / CREATIVE PRINCIPLES

Small ideas. Big possibilities.

A few useful starting points for making thoughtful visual work.

◉Contrast

Use differences in size, colour or weight to make the important parts stand out.

↔Balance

Distribute visual weight so a composition feels intentional, not accidental.

✳Repetition

Repeat shapes, spacing or colours to create rhythm and a recognisable visual system.

⌁Hierarchy

Guide the eye with a clear order: first, next and finally.
