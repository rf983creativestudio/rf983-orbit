03 / HOW TO THINK LIKE A SCIENTIST

Good questions lead
to better discoveries.

01

Observe

Notice what happens before deciding why it happens. Record what you can actually see or measure.

02

Ask

Turn curiosity into a clear question. What changes? What stays the same? What could explain it?

03

Test

Change one factor at a time where possible. Keep other conditions steady so comparisons are meaningful.

04

Reflect

Compare results with your prediction. Unexpected results are a reason to investigate, not to hide data.
