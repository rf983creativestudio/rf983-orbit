## Interactive Science Centre
- Six browser-based experiment stations: constant-speed motion, wave frequency and amplitude, Ohm's law circuit, planetary weight, RGB light mixing and particle states
- Ten-question science challenge with instant explanations and a restartable score
- Simulations run locally in the browser; controls and quiz answers are not submitted or saved
- Simplified educational models, not professional engineering tools


## ORBIT Creative Studio — Edition 01
- Browser-based creative workshop at `/creative/`
- Pattern Lab: generative geometric designs with adjustable styles, colour palettes, density and rotation
- Pixel Art Studio: touch/mouse drawing, palette swatches, grid sizing, undo and PNG export
- Poster Maker: editable title/subtitle, palette and composition presets with PNG export
- Colour Studio: HEX/RGB readout, generated five-step palettes, harmony swatches and PNG export
- Twelve rotating creative prompts and short visual-design principles
- No accounts, paid services or server-side artwork storage; tools run locally in the browser


## ORBIT Sound Lab — Edition 01
- Four browser-generated audio workspaces at `/sound/`
- Melody Keys: playable eight-note keyboard, waveform selection, octave control and sample scale
- Beat Builder: 16-step, four-lane sequencer with tempo, swing, starter rhythm and generated percussion
- Atmosphere Mixer: layered oscillator drone, modulated bell tone and filtered noise layer
- Sound Visualiser: live waveform display with adjustable oscillator type, frequency and volume
- Ten rotating sound missions and short explanations of frequency, amplitude, waveform and rhythm
- Uses Web Audio API only after user interaction; no streaming catalogue, microphone recording, account or server-side audio upload


## ORBIT Motion Studio — Edition 01
- Browser-based visual scene editor at `/motion/`
- Add circle, square, star and text layers; select, rename, recolour, resize, rotate, reposition and adjust opacity
- Drag scene objects on the canvas and edit their properties in the inspector
- Starter presets: blank canvas, orbiting planet, animated title and sunset shapes
- Keyframe timeline with smooth interpolation for position, size, rotation and opacity
- Preview, stop, scrub the timeline, export a PNG snapshot and record up to 8 seconds of WebM animation where supported
- Scene projects are stored locally in browser storage; no account, server-side project storage, microphone or audio capture


## ORBIT Video Studio — Edition 01
- Local browser-based video sequence editor at `/video/`
- Import multiple local video clips, arrange/reorder them and trim start/end points
- Preview the sequence, scrub the timeline and apply original, cinema, warm, cool, monochrome, vivid or dreamy filters
- Add a title overlay with adjustable text, colour, size and position
- Generate a short animated sample scene in supported browsers
- Export a still PNG or up to 60 seconds of WebM video where MediaRecorder and canvas capture are supported
- First edition exports picture only, without audio; footage is not uploaded and is processed locally in the browser

## ORBIT Command Centre — Unified Discovery Index
- Search and filter the published Knowledge Universe, World Encyclopedia, World Atlas, Earth Explorer and World Database from one page.
- Current index: 278 records — 24 learning guides, 48 encyclopedia entries, 49 country profiles, 10 separately classified territories / regions, 47 Earth field notes and 100 World Database records.
- Eight themed discovery routes help visitors explore related subjects across collections.
- Local bookmarks integrate with existing Encyclopedia, Earth Explorer and World Database saved lists; recent Command Centre visits are stored on the current device.
- Search runs locally in the browser. Collection data is loaded only on the Command Centre page, not on the homepage.
- Service worker v21 pre-caches a small site shell and the Command Centre; destination assets are cached when visitors open those destinations, reducing first-install work.
