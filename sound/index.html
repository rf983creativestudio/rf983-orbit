02 / UNDERSTAND SOUND

Sound is a moving story.

Every sound begins with vibration. The shape and speed of that vibration help shape what we hear.

〰Frequency

Frequency is the number of vibration cycles per second. It is measured in hertz (Hz) and strongly relates to perceived pitch.

◉Amplitude

Amplitude describes the size of a wave. In a simple sound model, greater amplitude generally means a louder signal.

∿Waveform

A waveform shows how a signal changes over time. Sine, square, triangle and sawtooth waves have different shapes and timbres.

♬Rhythm

Rhythm organises sounds and silences over time. Tempo is commonly measured in beats per minute (BPM).
